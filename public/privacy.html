Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

Smart Class Scheduling System ("the App", "we", "our") is a tool built for students and faculty to view class schedules. This page explains what information the App accesses, how it is used, and how it is protected.

1. Information We Access

When you sign in with your Google (.edu) account, the App requests access to:

  • Basic profile information — your name, email address, and profile picture, used only to identify you within the App and personalize your dashboard (e.g. "Good evening, [Name]!").
  • Read-only access to Google Sheets — used solely to retrieve class schedule data maintained by your section or program. The App does not create, edit, or delete any Google Sheets data.

2. Information Stored Locally

The App does not operate its own backend database. Information such as your selected section code and any personal activities/tasks you add are stored locally in your browser (via localStorage) and are not transmitted to or stored on any external server we control. Clearing your browser data or signing out will remove this information from your device.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and confirm you belong to a valid section.
  • To retrieve and display your class schedule from the linked Google Sheet.
  • To personalize the dashboard experience (greeting, today's classes, etc.).

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

4. Data Sharing

We do not share your Google account information with any third party. Data retrieved from Google Sheets is used only within your own browser session to render your schedule.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Since schedule and activity data lives in your browser's local storage, you can delete it at any time by signing out of the App, clearing your browser's site data for this domain, or using your browser's site settings.

6. Security

Sign-in is handled entirely through Google's OAuth 2.0 authentication flow. We never see or store your Google password. All requests to Google APIs are made over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

7. Third-Party Services

The App uses Google's Identity Services and Google Sheets API to function. Your use of these services is also subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for use by college/university students and faculty and is not directed at children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
